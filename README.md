# aave-market-analysis
Exploring liquidity, borrowing, utilization, and interest rates across Aave
## Why I chose this project

I chose Aave because its supply and borrow model fascinates me, and I’m interested in how decentralized finance could change the way we use financial services. I want to understand what happens inside the protocol and how people use it.

For my first analysis, I’ll focus on one Aave market and explore how much of each asset is supplied, how much is borrowed, and how utilization relates to interest rates.

## What I want to learn

I’m hoping to practice Python, Pandas, data cleaning, visualization, and reporting. My goal is to work with real data and explain my findings clearly while documenting what I learn along the way.

As I gain experience, I’d like to explore activity over time, active borrowing wallets, decentralized governance, differences between Aave versions, and comparisons with traditional finance.

## Project status

Just getting started. I’m defining the questions and learning how to collect and analyze the data.
